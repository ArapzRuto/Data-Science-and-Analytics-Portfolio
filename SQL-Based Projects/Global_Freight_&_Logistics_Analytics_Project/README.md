**Global Freight & Logistics PostgreSQL Analytics**

**Executive Overview**

This project presents analytical solution built natively in PostgreSQL to analyze global freight operations, delivery reliability, and trade corridor profitability. Leveraging  different SQL concepts including window functions, common table expressions (CTEs) and time-series lag functions,the project transforms raw shipping data into  actionable business intelligence.

**Objectives & Problem Statement**

Supply chain and logistics managers struggle to detect operational bottlenecks, cost overruns, and unexpected transit delays in real time. This PostgreSQL engine solves three core operational challenges:

1. Lead Time & Delay Tracking: Isolates delivery bottlenecks, rolling delay rates, and week-over-week lead time changes across logistics hubs.

2. Time-Series Financial Intelligence: Calculates 3-day, 7-day, 30-day, MTD, and 12-month rolling revenue, operating expense, and net profit margins.

2. Route & Corridor Optimization: Identifies peak revenue windows, trade corridor profitability, and gap-based sequence resets.
